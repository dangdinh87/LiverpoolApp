Lewis Koumas matches Steven Gerrard feat in the Champions League

10 hours ago

Lewis Koumas became only the second player aged 21 or younger to score and assist in a Champions League game for Liverpool during the 6-3 win at LASK.

By Glenn Price

Previous
Next

The Wales international set up Bradley Barcola's first goal on Wednesday evening before netting the Reds' sixth in the 72nd minute.

Steven Gerrard was the first to achieve that feat for the club, doing so away at Dynamo Kyiv back in October 2001.
