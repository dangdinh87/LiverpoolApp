In our latest guest blog featuring Liverpool-supporting musicians, Lewis Pike from The Cheap Thrills recounts his long-standing association with the Reds...

Ever since I can remember, it’s been Liverpool FC or nothing.

All four of us from The Cheap Thrills are massive Reds. I honestly don’t think it would have worked out if it was any different.

I was introduced to LFC and to football through my family. Liverpool as a city is football-mad, and kids have footy kits on pretty much from birth. I think there is something beautiful in that. It’s almost tribal, and my fate as a Liverpool fan was decided a long time before I was even born.

The 2000-01 season was a very special time, and I remember the buzz around the team and the city. Winning the treble that year showed a lot of us younger fans what it meant to be a Liverpool fan and to enjoy success. I was five or six at the time.

The first game I have a vague memory of going to (and a programme for evidence) was getting beaten in the League Cup against Grimsby during the 2001-02 season. I just remember being freezing and disappointed that we’d lost, but I was made up that I got to sing the Gary Mac song with my dad.

I started going to the match more regularly towards the end of Brendan Rodgers’ stint as manager. Fitzy, who plays bass, and his dad Danny were instrumental in making this possible for me. Danny had got us tickets for Manchester United at home in the FA Cup in 2012.

This was the first time we had our music played by George Sephton, and it was a really special moment being in the ground for it. Now it happens quite regularly, and it’s still a pinch-me moment every time. Me and Fitzy just stood there in the Upper Anfield Road smiling. Thousands of people around us, but none of them knew it was us playing the music we were listening to. I’d love to use this moment to thank George for his unwavering support of local music. Thank you, George!

My favourite game of all time is Barcelona at home in 2019. I worked in the bookies at the time and spent all day hyping up the customers who were putting 4-0 on. I did particularly well on tips over the next few days, with those same customers returning to pick up their winnings.
