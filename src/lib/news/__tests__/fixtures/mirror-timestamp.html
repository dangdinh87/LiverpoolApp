'I played with Robbie Fowler and Michael Owen – I know who was better Liverpool striker'

Robbie Fowler and Michael Owen have both had their say on the long-running debate

Steven Gerrard has reluctantly settled a debate over who was the best player - Robbie Fowler or Michael Owen. The argument over which of the two Liverpool striking legends was superior has rumbled on for years, with both Fowler and Owen themselves having had their say.

‌
‌

Fowler once told Simon Jordan's Up Front show that he was the better player, saying: "He was quicker than me, but in every other element of being a striker, I genuinely think I was better than him.

‌

"He could say the exact opposite but that is just my opinion and my self-confidence. I was better in every aspect of being a striker.‌ Michael wasn't the best player at Liverpool when I played, but I remember him coming through at the club and you could see that he was different class."

‌

As you might expect, Owen begged to differ, citing his record with both the Reds and England. He said: "I would expect him to say that.

"Listen, when I played for Liverpool, who did the manager pick? When I played for England, who did the manager pick? I mean, I don't have to say it – it was said for me."

Gerrard grew up idolising Fowler, watching him from The Kop before eventually lining up alongside him. Yet having spent more time on the pitch with Owen, the former Liverpool captain leans towards the ex-Real Madrid and Newcastle striker.

Gerrard told Open Goal: "[They're] very different. I would say Fowler was a better natural finisher. But in terms of sort of like fear, in terms of pace, movement... [Owen was superior]. I had a real top connection with Michael Owen; it was like we were just on a wavelength together.

‌

"Both had a period [when they were exceptional]. Up until Michael did his hamstring, he was world-class and up there with the best strikers in the world."
